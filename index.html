01 - About Me

Pradnil Birje

I am an aspiring Data Analyst focused on transforming raw, unstructured enterprise datasets into scalable visual data engines. Currently, I am expanding my technical foundation by building production-grade data pipelines, mastering robust database architectures, and modeling complex schemas.

Over the past two years, I mastered advanced SQL, schema modeling, and custom Power BI DAX infrastructure logic to drive measurable business impact. I successfully engineered a scalable pipeline to clean and stress-test over $8.34B in transactional data. This optimization allowed me to isolate $4.79B in critical margin metrics, significantly improving forecasting accuracy and data integrity.

Outside of writing optimized SQL window functions and fine-tuning dashboards, I am deeply driven by continuous learning at the intersection of technology and design. I dedicate my personal time to exercise, reading, improving myself as a person & spirituality.
