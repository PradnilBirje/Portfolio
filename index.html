About Me

I am an aspiring Data Analyst committed to building clean, high-integrity analytical environments. Rather than treating data as just numbers, I treat datasets like enterprise operational tools. My focus centers on mastering advanced database mechanics, executing rigorous exploratory data cleaning, and translating messy relational inputs into pristine visual metrics that guide corporate decision-making.

Technical Infrastructure Stack
